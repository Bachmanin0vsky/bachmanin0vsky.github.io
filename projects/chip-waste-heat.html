Thermal management

Chip cooling and waste heat recovery

Electronics cooling protects components from overheating. My undergraduate thesis explored whether a cooling system could also convert some of the heat from a chip into electricity.

Lab
Xi’an Jiaotong University, Yubing Tao’s group
Role
Undergraduate thesis researcher
Methods
COMSOL, thermal and thermoelectric modeling

Balancing cooling and electricity generation

I coupled a phase change material, which absorbs heat as it melts, with a thermoelectric module, which generates electricity from a temperature difference. I varied material, geometry, porosity, and operating conditions in COMSOL.

I compared chip temperature stability and predicted power generation across the simulated designs. The two metrics guided my choice of configurations that could keep the chip cooler while recovering some of its waste heat.
