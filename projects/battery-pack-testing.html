Industry experience

Battery pack safety testing

Battery pack safety depends on how the pack and its controls respond to water exposure and electrical faults. I supported pack and control-board testing during an internship at Ningbo Deye Technology.

Company
Ningbo Deye Technology Co., Ltd.
Role
Test department intern, summer 2024
Methods
Pack testing, voltage and current transient analysis

Running tests and interpreting responses

I carried out waterproofing and short-circuit tests on battery packs, recorded voltage and current over time, and analyzed the traces to support safety assessments. I also tested software and hardware on the high- and low-voltage sides of battery management system control boards.
